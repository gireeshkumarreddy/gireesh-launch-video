SONARFEEL EVERY FREQUENCY.
01 / THE STARTING POINT

Turn technical performance into an immediate feeling.

Audio products have a lot to explain: materials, comfort, sound quality, and everyday usability. SONAR explores how to communicate that depth without turning the experience into a wall of specifications.

Made for listeners who see their headphones as part of their daily world—from a focused work session to the walk home.

“You don’t need to hear the product to feel its personality.”
THE DESIGN PRINCIPLE
02 / THE DIRECTION

Give the experience a rhythm of its own.

Cobalt, chrome, and sharp lime accents establish a confident identity. A modular story moves between full-product moments and quieter explanations, giving the experience contrast and pace.

  • Start with a feeling, then support it with useful detail.
  • Use motion to connect product features to real moments.
  • Keep the product and the next decision visible throughout.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

A clear path.
A distinct feeling.

The concept uses progressive storytelling: meet the product, understand the listening experience, explore the details, and choose the right option. Motion supports that order rather than competing with it.

WHAT THE CONCEPT BRINGS TOGETHER

Art direction

A bold product identity with a cohesive campaign language.

Motion studies

Entrance, transition, and scroll-story interaction concepts.

Web experience

A responsive product narrative and reusable section system.

04 / THE TAKEAWAY

A note for
the next idea.

Motion works best when it has a job. Giving each transition a clear purpose creates more impact than keeping everything moving all the time.

This is an independent design exploration. It presents the intended experience and design direction, rather than results from a launched product.
