A NEW PERSPECTIVE ON EVERYDAY
01 / THE STARTING POINT

Make shopping feel like discovering your next favourite thing.

Fashion commerce often asks a brand to choose between expression and ease. The concept for FORME explores how a distinctive editorial identity can sit alongside a clear, familiar shopping journey.

Designed for people who care about the details: the shape of a bag, the texture of leather, and the story behind the things they choose to keep.

“A store should have a point of view. The interface should help you find yours.”
THE DESIGN PRINCIPLE
02 / THE DIRECTION

Less visual noise. More room for the product.

A warm ochre surface, sculptural product imagery, and a restrained burgundy palette create a recognizable visual language. The layout gives each piece room to breathe without losing the structure of a useful shop.

  • Lead with a strong product story, then make browsing feel effortless.
  • Give product information the same care as campaign imagery.
  • Keep navigation, selection, and the purchase path predictable.

Editorial / Precise

Expressive serif headlines meet clear, quiet interface typography. Contrast creates character; consistency keeps the experience usable.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

A clear path.
A distinct feeling.

The journey moves from an expressive collection introduction to focused product discovery. Product details, material information, and purchasing decisions stay close together so people can move at their own pace.

WHAT THE CONCEPT BRINGS TOGETHER

Visual direction

A focused palette, campaign treatment, and editorial type hierarchy.

Shopping journey

Collection, product-detail, and purchase-flow concepts.

Responsive system

Flexible layouts and components that work across screen sizes.

04 / THE TAKEAWAY

A note for
the next idea.

The strongest direction came from separating expression from friction. A shop can feel distinctive without making its visitors learn a new way to shop.

This is an independent design exploration. It presents the intended experience and design direction, rather than results from a launched product.
