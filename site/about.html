A LITTLE MORE ABOUT THE PERSON

Curiosity first.
Always.

Designer, visual storyteller, and a firm believer that the best ideas usually start with a good question.

I’m Gireesh. I turn complex ideas into clear, expressive digital experiences.

My way into design started with an interest in how people see things: the image that makes you stop, the story that stays with you, and the small detail that changes how something feels.

Today, I bring that instinct to interfaces. I like the space where good structure and a strong visual point of view meet—where an experience can be easy to use and impossible to confuse with anything else.

With roots in India and a life in France, I’m always collecting perspectives. Different places, conversations, films, and everyday observations all find their way into the work.
